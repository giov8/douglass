How to Speed-Up Your Essay Writing Process?

In advanced academic classes, such as universities, there are several essays that students are assigned to complete. Students must complete each of the different essays in the allotted time. Each student goes through a phase where they must juggle several essays or more at once. During this time, it makes sense to tweak and speed up the essay process a bit so that students can deliver each essay on time despite the workload. For students in higher grades, it is important to learn how to speed up the essay process. It also helps them when they are burdened by exams, assignments, and tests. Below is an essay writing guide that will allow you to speed up the essay writing process:

Understand the essay assignment

The assignment should be understood and checked carefully because any misunderstanding at this point can cost you your whole essay. Paraphrasing the assignment in your own words is the best way to understand what the assignment wants, what kind of text you should write, and what the topic is.

Brainstorming with the help of background research

Although there are many brainstorming techniques used by writing essay companies, you should use mind maps as they are most useful for the last parts of the essay. Mind mapping allows you to jot down your ideas, show connections, and see the hierarchy of information. This, along with the background information you've gleaned from subject encyclopedia entries, will also help you create the essay outline.

Use a known database for research

When you are searching for research articles to get information and evidence for your essays, you should stick to a database that you are familiar with. This will help you find sources more accurately and quickly. You can then narrow your search even further by reading the abstracts of the articles and papers.

Take notes and expand on the brainstorming notes

Keep adding new information to the mind map that you come across. This will help you more easily recall the information for the writing process.

Hurry up with the draft of your essay

You need to get your essay in shape as soon as possible. The essay should be written without much consideration for errors that occur at both the macro and micro levels. You should expand the rough outline you created through brainstorming and summarize the useful information into sentences and paragraphs.

Have someone review it

Usually, the revision process spans several iterations. However, if you are in a hurry, try to complete your revision in three steps: Group similar ideas into individual paragraphs so that you develop consistency in each paragraph. Make sure that each paragraph has a topic sentence that transitions the essay. Arrange the information according to the proper flow of the argument, starting with the premise, moving through the evidence and analysis, and ending with the conclusion of the paragraph. It is also useful to place the important points at the beginning of the essay. Remove weak paragraphs or merge them into stronger paragraphs.

Editing and proofreading right after

For this task, it is best to take the help of a person who offers writing services for your essay. A colleague, friend, or even a family member can point out mistakes in your writing style since they are not familiar with the essay. You can further speed up this process by consulting various editing and proofreading software available on the internet. Submit shortly after You can submit your essay just before the deadline, but you should not leave it to the last minute. It is best to submit it with a time cushion of a few hours.
